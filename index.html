Our Purpose

Clarity before
capital is committed

Pharos Property Advisors is a client-side, advisory and coordination specialist.

Pharos Property Advisors help institutional owners and managers of Build-to-Rent, PRS, student accommodation and hotels to make higher-risk buildings (HRBs) fundable, compliant and market-ready.

We provide insurance backed non-technical, client-side oversight that turns fragmented technical outputs into a coherent, lender-ready programme of action. This reduces risk, clarifies responsibilities, and enables remediation funding.

The post-Grenfell regulatory and commercial landscape requires robust building safety documentation, demonstrable "golden thread" controls, and fundable remediation plans. Pharos Property Advisors bridges the gap between siloed technical reports and delivery by coordinating specialists, auditing documentation, and managing the client-side programme so stakeholders can rely on a clear route to compliance.

Services

Non-technical oversight that delivers results

01

Building Safety Documentation Audit

Structured audit and gap analysis of drawings, O&Ms, fire strategy (FRA/FRAEW), external wall evidence and resident records, delivering a clear route to Building Safety compliance.

02

Golden Thread Management

Define viable golden thread information, create a defensible data room with version control, ownership mapping and traceability to meet the Building Safety Regulator (BSR), lender and insurer expectations.

03

Competent Appointment & Assessment Oversight

Draft scopes, run competitive appointments, manage programmes and outputs for fire engineers, façade specialists and duty-holder roles — ensuring clarity, completeness and coordination.

04

Remediation Strategy

Options appraisal, cost/programme/risk modelling, procurement strategy and stakeholder coordination; client-side programme management for delivery and funder engagement.

05

Purchase & Sale Advisory

Due-diligence review of building safety documentation at sale or purchase, with an executable plan to address gaps and reduce transaction risk.
